Story time! Fr. Elric is currently the parish priest in Nuestra Senora de Guadalupe, Lantapan, Bukidnon. My first interaction with Padre is sa isa ka PPC meeting, we talked about his passion in his daily reflection vlogs called “Wanminit with Fr. Elric.”

Gaka-amaze ko sa passion ni Padre because of the commitment to post daily, and to post not just mediocre videos but reflection with intentions, kanang dili mema nga video, pero gina preparahan gyud. Gaka-amaze ko kai I was once posted daily short reflections, and I know grabe kalisod ang mahimong consistent.

Then late Pope Francis canonized Blessed Carlo Acutis. And then I discovered, St. Carlo built a digital catalog for Eucharistic Miracles (https://www.miracolieucaristici.org/). Ayusa oy! Simple lang kaayo ang idea ba, pero impactful. So I challenged myself, not to become a saint (hopefully noh), but to built something with impact (well dili jud same impact with St. Carlo 🤣).

Then I remembered, when I visited the shrine of Venerable Teofilo Camomot (Servant of God that time) in Carcar Cebu, nitatak sa akoa ang compilation of his homilies. 

**_“Grabe ba, naa juy naka collect ug nihipos sa iyang homilies.”_**

So I decided mag build ko og compilation of homilies. But the question is, kang kinsang homily? 🤣 Then I remembered Fr. Elric and his daily one-minute reflection vlogs.

“Why not I build a repository of his vlogs, so that kung ma santos na si Padre, anyone can access his daily reflections.” So I build it, I approached Padre, and offered it as a gift for his passion.

That’s it, the very reason I built Wanminit w/ Fr. Elric Web because I want the faithful to easily access his inspiring reflection videos. So that when time comes, a repository of his good works through social media will stay forever.

Story time! Remember my story about Why I built DailyAsk? About my conviction?

**_“I must use my skills for the faithful.”_**

So huna-huna napud ko, unsa napuy chada ibuild. 🤔🤔🤔. This is in June. Then I remembered, hapit na ang death anniversary ni Lola Gleva. I remembered 2020, during pandemic, we gathered online to pray 9 days novena para kang Lola, ug ako pagyud ang mananabtan (prayer leader) 🤣. Core memory to sya sa ako kai aside sa first time nako mahimong mananabtan, it was an intimate moment na maskin dili physically present ang uban sa amo, we felt their presence online for the novena.

So I built Cenacle, a sacred space for anyone to gather in prayer. A video conferencing tool built specifically for prayer. May it be novena, rosary, 10,000 Hail Marys, etc. Ug puhon, we will have daily online Mass.

“Wait, diba naa namai Zoom? Google Meet?” Well, I built Cenacle to be very user friendly, no account needed for the joiners (only for room creators). Create and schedule a room and share link. For joiners, click the link and join the room. Simple as that.

That’s it, the very reason I built Cenacle because I want families, barkada, community, or any group of people to have a sacred platform to gather to pray, may it be novena, rosary, 10,000 Hail Mary’s, etc.

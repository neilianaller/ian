Story time! Every time I build a system or an app that needs SMS features, perme ko mag hesitate to implement sa feature kay almost paid services, maskin cents lang per message, pero still costly for indie developers.

Then naka-hunahuna ko, **_“Kaya ra kaha magbuild kog akoa? Ako lang gud mag-gamit.”_**

**Here it is, SMSAPI, turn your Android phone into a powerful SMS Gateway. Send, track, and manage messages through a secure API and real-time dashboard.**

Imong capstone, imong project, imong phone, imong SIM, imong load, imong data, imong integration. 

That’s it, the very reason I built SMSAPI for devs building their side and personal projects (in development phase, pwede pud sa production pero not advisable), for students building their capstone projects, na dili maka afford for paid SMS API services.

Story time! As a parent, very scary ang moments na magkasakit ang anak. Kulba ug kabalaka ga-abot. And every checkup, naay question na perme mi dili makasagang.

**_“Unsa last antibiotic ani ni baby? Kay dili baya ta pwede mag reseta og the same level na antibiotic within 3 months.”_**

At tumigil ang mundo……. Shocks, unsa gani to? Something “cin” man gyud to.

Bi, mag tinamdan ta, atong hipuson ang mga reseta. Atong i-tapok sa baby book. Time flies, dili na magkasakit, back to normal gaming. And then, “Sakit is waving again.”

_“Asa gani to nabutang ang baby book?” “Unsa gani petsaha atong last checkup?” Nawala napud._

And then Cacay asked me, _“What if maghimo ka og app Pap na ma track nato ang medications ni BBL?”_

Bitaw ba? Pero wait, before ko magbuild, mag research sa ko kai basin naa na, gakapoy-kapoy rakog himo nya naa na diay. I searched in Play Store and Google for any medication tracker. And I found many apps, and I noticed the following

**“Needs internet connection”, “With so many ads”, “Not so very user friendly”.**

Then I decided, “Sige, himo ta, kami ra bitaw maggamit”. 

“Offline first”, “Ad free”, “Easy to use”.
-

And guess what kinsang profile ang unang na-add? 🙋‍♂️(Ako, haha). Nagkasakit ko 🤣. Ako naman nuon ang una nabutangan og medication instead of si BBL 🤣. Then nagpaibot kog ngipon, I used MedTrack to inform our dentist na naa koi antibiotic month before sa pagpaibot.

So in short, yes, it’s working. It helped me, it helped us. And I am offering this to the world (chaaar), to you 🫶.

That’s it, the very reason I built MedTrack is to help parents and family members to track medication for the family.

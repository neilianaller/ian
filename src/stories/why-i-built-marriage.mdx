Story time! Sometime in May, adtong naa mi sa hospital kai isa ko sa nagbantay sa akong mother-in-law. Then during those time, na operahan pud akong mama sa Cebu. Through those incidents, God reminded me of how always be beautiful marriage is. Both my papa and ugangan gi-ubanan ilang mga spouse sa hospital, knowing all will be alright in time.

**_Then I remembered, during the reception sa among kasal ni Cacay, I made a promise to serve her always, and she promised to love me always and in all ways._**

That’s it, the very reason I built Marriage is Beautiful web app because I was inspired to share how beautiful marriage is. With the lots of people afraid of commitment nowadays, I hope this platform will help realize them that Marriage is not scary, it’s beautiful.

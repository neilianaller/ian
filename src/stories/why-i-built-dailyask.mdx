## The Spark

I've always been drawn to spiritual practices that invite quiet reflection—especially the daily Gospel reading. There's something powerful about pausing your day to sit with a passage and ask yourself: *What is God saying to me today?*

But I noticed something frustrating: most devotional apps are cluttered. They're filled with ads, social features, notifications that don't respect your peace. They *want* you to spend time in them, not with the text itself.

I wanted to build the opposite. An app that respects your time. An app that gets out of the way.

## The Decision

DailyAsk started as a personal project—just a way to get the daily Gospel reading on my phone without all the noise. But as I kept using it, I realized others might want this too.

So I built it:
- **No ads.** Ever. The app doesn't try to monetize your attention.
- **Offline-first.** Your readings are stored locally. No internet required.
- **Intentional.** One reading per day. One prompt: *What is God saying to me today?* The rest is silence.

## The Impact

What surprised me most wasn't the technical challenge—it was the feedback. People told me they were having real conversations with their faith again. Parents were using it with their kids. Small communities were gathering around the same daily readings.

That's when I understood: the best apps aren't the ones that maximize engagement. They're the ones that respect the user. They do one thing, and they do it with care.

DailyAsk will always be free, always ad-free, and always focused on reflection over distraction.

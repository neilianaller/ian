Story time! Ever since I learned how to build apps, I have this conviction,

**_“I must use this skill for the faithful.”_**

So then I realized, _“Usual na struggle is prayer life, sa ka busy ug sa kadaghan og mga ginabuhat sa life, malimot ta mag-ampo.”_ 

**_“And with so many apps already available, unsa pa man akong ma contribute? Kanang subtle lang nga mag remind sa user nga mag pray. Nga kanang inig open sa user sa iyang phone, mag remind na sa iya deretso.”_**

So then I build **DailyAsk**. A tool designed for the daily Catholic life. The app brings you the official liturgical Gospel reading (NABRE) paired with a unique, Al-generated reflection to help you start a prayer and reflection.
 All you need to do is to add DailyAsk Widget to your home screen. The reflection question will be in your home screen and notification.

That’s it, the very reason I built DailyAsk is to remind us to pause and take time to pray. This helped me especially sa times na lisod mag-ampo atong nahospital si Cacay. I hope this will help you too. Kabalo ko busy kaayo atong adlaw, daghan tag ginabuhat, maong simple reminder lang ni.

Story time! Last week of December 2025, one workmate asked if kaya ba nako maka himo og registration platform para sa upcoming running event na managed by LGU. Their event is scheduled on February ug mag post na sila sa socmed for promotions within a week sa iyang pag-reach out sa ako. I told her, “Kaya ra mam pero needs time. Dili nako ma-apas inyong timeline maam.”

After that conversation, sige nag dagan sa akong huna-huna, “Why not bitaw ba? Maghimo kaha kog platform for running events.” So then I explored if naa nay mga platform na nag exist, which is daghan na kaayo.

**“So nganung maghimo pako?” My answer, “Why not?” 🤣**


So then I built it, code every day and night. Code diri, code didto. Laptop diri, laptop didto. And here come’s the last part, launching. 

**_“Dili nalang nako ni dayunon bai, basin dili ni mulungtad. Basin ma flop ni. Unsaon lugar nako pagmarket ani sa mga organizers? Naa pud kahay mupansin ani?” Here comes the doubts. 🤔Then I stopped doing it. “Dili gyud siguro ni para sa ako.”_**

Then here comes the event atong akong workmate na ilang gina organize. I learned na ang ilang gikuha na organizer is the same organizer atong na asikaso nako last Lantapan Run 2025. So medyo nagka friend mi 🤣. Nag pa backer ko sa akong friend na ma introduce ko sa ila, aaaand, karemember pa sila sa ako, slight. So with my laptop, while kamulo silag setup sa arc and everything else, I presented RunnersPod to them, lo and behold, interested sila. We exchanged conversations and plans, and the rest is history. They have been using RunnersPod for their past 7 events and they have 6 events with registration currently open in the platform.

So that’s it, that’s the very reason why I built RunnersPod is to help organizer managed registration hassle free. No more manual checking, no more follow up sa registrants kung nasend na ang screenshot, so on and so forth. Also for the runners na dali ra mag register, nig bayad confirmed diretso ang slot, dili na mag screenshot og isend pa sa organizer.
-

And also, basin maka pangutana mo, “Nganung gidayon gihapon nako og launch?” Well, I hate regrets. “Dili ko gusto na one day, maghinuktok ko nga nagmahay nganung wala nako gilahos.”

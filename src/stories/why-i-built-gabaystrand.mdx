Story time! One day, I said to myself, “Dapat naa pud koy ma build na for students ba. Kanang to help them as a student.” So I explored what apps or platforms I can offer for the students. Ga brainstorm ko with Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT kung unsa-unsa ang mga apps na for elementary, high school or college.

When we are exploring for the elementary, ana ko, “Lisod man maghimo og app sa ila, kay meaning maghatag tag chance sa ila na maggamit sa phone, sa kabata pa nila, dili sha advisable.” So we moved on to high school, daghan og ideas ang AI but one common suggestion nila is a platform for high school students to interact with other students, mura shag social networking for students. And for college, we talked about study guides, pomodoro, and other tools to help them study. Ana ko, “Daghan naman ing-ani available online, dili nako mag-add pa sa kadaghan.”

Then, eureka moment 😲! How about a tool for students na maghelp sa ila kung unsa ilang course for college. Because I can still remember, after my high school, wala koy idea kung unsa akong kuhaon na course for college. Basta ang sure sa ako, dili ko sa Cebu City mag college, sa Toledo ra. Then I took entrance exam sa CCTC, and the only options were Education, IT, and HRM. Dili ko ganahan mag maestra, ako pud huna-huna nga pambabae ang HRM (which is dili diay), so I chose IT. And the rest is history. Looking back now, I realized, God made me chose IT, kay kung wala ko nag IT, wala nako ma experience ang akong gaka experience karon.

And so while building, na realize nako, very wide man kaayo kung ang options is course, like daghan kaayog course. So atras ko gamay and instead of guidance for choosing a course, why not for choosing Senior High Strand usa.

That’s it, the very reason I built GabayStrand is to help junior high students na maka decide kung unsa ang ilang kuhaon na strand. The result of the assessment will be based on their answers to set of questions. 5 topics, 15 questions, 5 minutes.
